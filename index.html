RAG assistant for plant SOPs

Business problemOperators lose time hunting through long SOPs and policies for one answer, and a wrong answer on safety or quality is costly.

Sample data for demonstration

Extractive answer

Top 3 retrieved passages

    Query your own text (stays in your browser)

    Browse the 10 sample documents

    How it works

    Each document is split into short passages. Every passage becomes a TF-IDF vector: words that are frequent in that passage but rare across the collection get more weight. Your question is turned into the same kind of vector, and passages are ranked by cosine similarity. The answer is extractive. It quotes the best-matching sentences from the top passages and cites them, so it cannot make up facts.

    How this scales at a client

    • Swap TF-IDF for dense embeddings plus a vector store, keep keyword search for exact terms (hybrid retrieval), and add an LLM that must answer only from cited passages.
    • Connect to the document management system with role-based access, version tracking and an evaluation set of real operator questions to measure answer accuracy before rollout.

    Agentic procurement workflow

    Business problemBuyers check stock, compare suppliers and raise purchase orders by hand, so stock-outs slip through and supplier choices are inconsistent.

    Sample data for demonstration

      How it works

      The agent is rule-based but built as a real agent loop. It writes a plan, then repeatedly picks a tool (read inventory, check reorder point, score suppliers, draft PO, check policy), reads the result, and decides the next step. Suppliers are scored on min-max normalised cost, lead time and risk using your weights. If an item will run out before the best supplier can deliver, the agent re-plans and picks the fastest acceptable supplier. Nothing is ordered until a human approves.

      How this scales at a client

      • Replace the rules with an LLM planner that calls the same tools through ERP APIs (stock, vendor master, contract prices), keeping hard-coded policy checks and approval limits around it.
      • Log every thought, tool call and approval for audit, start in "draft only" mode, and measure stock-outs avoided and buyer hours saved before allowing auto-release of low-value orders.

      Supply-chain knowledge graph

      Business problemWhen a supplier fails, teams struggle to see quickly which components, products and plants are exposed, and which suppliers are single points of failure.

      How it works

      Suppliers, components, products and plants are nodes; "supplies", "used in" and "made at" are edges. A disruption propagates downstream: a component is lost if all its suppliers are down, or at risk if only some are. A product is lost if any of its components is lost. Shortest paths use breadth-first search. Criticality ranks suppliers by how many products they would take down, with betweenness centrality (how often a node sits on shortest paths) as a tie-breaker.

      How this scales at a client

      • Load supplier master, BOMs and plant routings from ERP into a graph database (for example Neo4j) and enrich it with tier-2 suppliers, locations and risk feeds.
      • Run the same impact query on live events (port closures, quality holds) to trigger alerts and dual-sourcing reviews for the highest-centrality suppliers.

      Demand forecasting

      Business problemPlanners need a defensible monthly demand forecast with an honest range, so they can set production and safety stock without over- or under-building.

      Sample data for demonstration
      Data

      Hold-out comparison (computed live)
      MethodHold-out MAPEFitted parameters

      How it works

      Holt-Winters keeps three running estimates: the level, the trend and a seasonal adjustment for each month, and updates each one a little every month (alpha, beta, gamma). The smoothing weights are chosen by grid search to minimise one-step-ahead error. To be honest about accuracy, the last 12 months (a quarter of the data for short uploads) are hidden, the model is fitted on the rest, and MAPE (mean absolute percentage error) is measured on that hidden period. The shaded band is an approximate 95% range that widens with the horizon. CSV upload: one value column, optionally with a date column.

      How this scales at a client

      • Run the same pipeline for thousands of SKU-location series in BigQuery or Python, with automatic method selection by back-test error and gradient-boosted models where drivers (price, promotions) matter.
      • Publish forecasts and error tracking to Power BI or Tableau so planners can override with a reason, and measure forecast value-add against the naive baseline every cycle.

      Manufacturing quality monitor (SPC)

      Business problemQuality teams often find a drifting process only after a batch fails. They need an early signal while the product can still be saved.

      Sample data for demonstration
      In control

      Rule violations

        How it works

        A simulated viscosity sensor produces subgroups of 5 readings. Control limits come from the first 25 in-control subgroups (X-bar chart: grand mean ± A2·R̄; range chart: D3·R̄ to D4·R̄). Each new point is checked against four Western Electric rules: one point beyond 3σ, 2 of 3 beyond 2σ, 4 of 5 beyond 1σ, or 8 in a row on one side. The Pareto chart ranks defect causes so the few that drive most defects get fixed first. This is the same idea as the early-warning framework for product instability I proposed during my L'Oréal R&D internship: flag a trend in lab measurements before it becomes a failed product.

        How this scales at a client

        • Stream real sensor and LIMS data (OPC-UA, MES) into a time-series store, compute limits per product and line, and push rule violations to operators and quality engineers in real time.
        • Link signals to batch records and defect codes so the Pareto updates itself, and add multivariate or ML anomaly detection where many correlated parameters move together.

        Vision: hand-gesture recognition

        Business problemOn the shop floor, gloved or busy hands make touchscreens awkward. Touch-free gestures could confirm steps or call help.

        Runs on device

        Privacy: the camera starts only when you press the button. Video frames and uploaded images are processed on your device and never leave it. Only the open-source model files are downloaded from a public CDN.

        Recognised gesture

        -

        How it works

        MediaPipe Hand Landmarker finds 21 points on each hand (wrist, knuckles, finger tips). A finger counts as extended when its tip is further from the wrist than its middle joint; the thumb is compared against the base of the little finger. The pattern of extended fingers maps to a gesture. Before you start the camera, the same classifier runs on built-in sample landmark sets so you can see the output. It builds on Gestural Music (Shaastra, Team Envisage), where finger tracking with OpenCV and MediaPipe was mapped to MIDI notes.

        How this scales at a client

        • Run on edge devices at workstations to confirm SOP steps or flag missing PPE, keeping video on the device and only sending events.
        • Train custom gesture or object models on site images, then validate under real lighting, gloves and camera angles before any operational use.

        How I work

        Plan, Do, Check, Act

        The improvement cycle I learned in process engineering is how I run analytics work too.

        1. P

          Plan

          Frame the decision before touching data.

          ExampleIn the EV charging case, I sized TAM, SAM and SOM (43K+ public charging points) and ranked states before recommending Karnataka as the pilot.

        2. D

          Do

          Build the smallest model that answers the question.

          ExampleAt L'Oréal, I built a PLS regression scoring model linking lab measurements to consumer-perceived quality, to replace a manual panel test.

        3. C

          Check

          Test against reality and find what really limits the result.

          ExampleIn my CO2 absorption research with MDEA, I first found the rate-limiting step (interfacial area) before evaluating any fix.

        4. A

          Act

          Turn findings into a standing process or a clear call.

          ExampleAfter stress-testing 6 formulations from 5°C to 55°C, I proposed an early-warning framework for product instability. For the CO2 work, the Pickering-emulsion evaluation ends in a go/no-go call.
